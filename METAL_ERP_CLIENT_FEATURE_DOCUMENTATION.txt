1. EXECUTIVE SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Apex Metal ERP is a specialized, production-ready enterprise software 
engineered specifically for the metal melting and rolling mill manufacturing sector.

Unlike generic ERPs or simple accounting software, this system addresses the 
unique, high-stakes operational challenges of metal manufacturing:
- Liquid metal furnace melting with extreme thermal loss.
- Slag generation and recoverable mill scale / crop-end scrap.
- Rolling mill pass reduction and bundle weight management.
- Dynamic conversion of scrap materials into high-tensile TMT rebars.
- Multi-store inventory isolation (Raw, WIP Billets, Finished Goods, Scrap).
- Strict GST tax compliance (CGST, SGST, IGST with HSN codes).
- Integrated double-entry accounting tied directly to physical shop-floor events.

The software features an executive White & Blue Frosted Glass aesthetic 
(Glassmorphism) with high contrast, optimized for viewing on factory floor tablets, 
control cabin touchscreens, and executive desktop workstations.


2. CORE DIFFERENTIATOR: AUTOMATIC WEIGHT LOSS & BURNING LOSS ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In metal melting and rolling, tracking material weight loss is the primary driver 
of factory profitability. A 0.5% unexplained burning loss can cost a mill 
lakhs of rupees monthly.

The Apex Metal ERP features an automatic, real-time Weight Loss Engine:

A. Weight Loss Mathematical Formulation:
   - Process Loss (KG) = Input Weight - Finished Output Weight - Recoverable Scrap
   - Loss Percentage (%) = (Process Loss / Input Weight) * 100
   - Finished Yield (%) = (Finished Output Weight / Input Weight) * 100
   - Scrap Recovery (%) = (Recoverable Scrap Weight / Input Weight) * 100

B. Real-Time Unit Production Cost Engine:
   Total Manufacturing Cost = 
       Input Material Cost (FIFO Batch Rate)
     + Labour Wages & Overtime
     + Electricity / Power Units Consumed
     + Furnace Fuel (Furnace Oil / Coal / Gas)
     + Consumables, Chemicals & Mill Overheads

   Unit Cost per Finished KG = Total Manufacturing Cost / Finished Output Weight

C. Tolerance Guardrails & Audit Warnings:
   - Standard Factory Tolerance: 2.0% to 3.5% burning loss.
   - If an operator enters an order where loss exceeds the upper threshold (e.g. >3.5%), 
     the system highlights an amber/red warning banner requiring explicit supervisor 
     and metallurgical quality sign-off before closing the heat order.


3. STANDARD 100 KG -> 97 KG VERIFICATION SCENARIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prove the mathematical accuracy and end-to-end integration of the system to 
your client, the software comes pre-loaded with a "Golden Benchmark Scenario":

- Step 1: Inward 100.00 KG of Mild Steel Melting Scrap at ₹100.00 / KG.
          Total Raw Material Value = ₹10,000.00 (+ GST ₹1,800 + Freight ₹350 = ₹12,150).
- Step 2: Transfer 100.00 KG into Induction Furnace Heat #HEAT-2026-089.
- Step 3: Run Melting & Rolling Process:
          * Finished Output (Fe-500D 12mm TMT Rebars): 97.00 KG (97.0% Yield)
          * Recoverable Mill Scrap (Scale / Runners):   0.00 KG (or customizable)
          * Actual Burning Loss:                       3.00 KG (3.00% Loss)
          * Tolerance Status:                          3.00% is WITHIN 2.0% - 3.5% range.
- Step 4: Overheads Applied to Heat:
          * Labour Cost:       ₹1,500.00
          * Electricity Cost:  ₹2,800.00
          * Fuel Cost:         ₹850.00
          * Other Overheads:   ₹350.00
          * Total Heat Cost:   ₹5,500.00
- Step 5: Finished Good Valuation:
          * Effective Cost per KG of Finished Rebar = ₹56.70 / KG.
          * Finished goods automatically added to Finished Goods Store.
          * Raw Material batch stock deducted by exactly 100.00 KG.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULE C: 4-STORE REAL-TIME INVENTORY SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physical metal mills require strict isolation between material states. The system 
enforces 4 separate digital stores:

1. Raw Material Store:
   Heavy melting scrap, turning scrap, sponge iron, DRI, and ferro-alloys.
2. WIP Billets Store:
   Hot cast billets, cold stock billets, blooms, and ingots.
3. Finished Goods Store:
   Thermo-Mechanically Treated (TMT) rebars (8mm, 10mm, 12mm, 16mm, 20mm, 25mm, 32mm), 
   structural angles, wire rods, and channels.
4. Recoverable Scrap Store:
   Mill scale, crop ends, runner heads, and cobbles that can be recharged into future heats.

Features in Inventory:
- Batch Traceability: Every batch records inward date, initial weight, available weight, 
  cost rate, and heat reference.
- Double-Entry Stock Movement Ledger: Every gram of metal inwarded, transferred, consumed, 
  or dispatched is logged with source document ID, before/after balances, and user stamps.
- Stock Adjustment Facility: Authorised manual stock adjustments with mandatory 
  audit reasons (e.g., Physical Verification Surplus/Deficit).
- Negative Stock Prevention: Strictly blocks dispatches or furnace charging beyond 
  physical stock unless an override is authorized.

MODULE F: DOUBLE-ENTRY ACCOUNTING & FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike lightweight inventory tools that require external Tally or Zoho, this ERP 
has a native, double-entry general ledger:

- Balanced Journal Entries: Automatically created on Purchase, Manufacturing, Sales, 
  and Cash/Bank transactions. Enforces Debit = Credit.
- Chart of Accounts: Categorized into Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses.
- Real-Time Financial Statements:
  1. Trial Balance: Complete list of all ledger accounts with balanced debit and credit totals.
  2. Profit & Loss Statement (P&L):
     * Gross Sales Revenue
     * Cost of Goods Sold (Raw Material + Direct Labour + Furnace Power + Fuel + Overheads)
     * Gross Margin
     * Net Operating Profit
  3. Balance Sheet: Real-time accounting of Total Current/Fixed Assets vs. Current Liabilities & Equity.
- Payment & Receipt Vouchers:
  * Record vendor payments and customer receipt vouchers.
  * Multiple payment modes: Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, Cheque, Cash in Hand.
  * Tracks bank reference numbers and narration notes.

5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & DATA INTEGRITY SAFEGUARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Next.js 14 App Router: High-speed rendering, zero lag, smooth transitions.
- Client-Side React Context Architecture: Runs completely offline in the browser; 
  zero reliance on external database servers during sales demos.
- LocalStorage Persistence: Any change (new purchase, manufacturing order, stock 
  adjustment, sale, or voucher) is instantly saved to the client's local storage.
- Atomic Stock Transactions: An order either completes fully (deducting raw material, 
  adding finished good, and posting accounting) or fails cleanly without orphan records.
- Export Capabilities:
  * PDF Generator: jsPDF + jsPDF-AutoTable for GST Tax Invoices.
  * Excel Generator: SheetJS (XLSX) for financial and operational spreadsheets.
